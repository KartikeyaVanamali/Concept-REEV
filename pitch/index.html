Independent concept · no hardware

What if the XEV 9S could go anywhere?

A series plug-in REEV on Mahindra’s INGLO skateboard. Daily driving is electric. The engine never turns a wheel. Charging stops do not decide the trip.

First-principles planning ranges. Not affiliated with Mahindra or MG.

The question

Remove the charging veto. Keep what the 9S already does well.

This is not a clean-sheet car and not a trim change. It is a derivative programme: half the pack, a generator in the nose, a control law that treats battery, engine, regen, and recovery as one system.

Who it is for

The ICE and diesel buyer — not the BEV believer.

  • Diesel’s metro age clock, DPF, AdBlue.
  • 40% GST on a large strong hybrid that still burns petrol every day.
  • A weekday petrol bill at the engine’s worst efficiency point.
  • A BEV owner with a home charger and a short commute should buy the 9S.

Hardware

Always-electric drive. 40 / 50 kWh. 105 kW generator.

  • RWD 170 kW · AWD 240 kW · 42 L tank · 400–465 V INGLO bus.
  • Six fixed HV-DC bands: 30 / 40 / 55 / 70 / 90 / 105 kW.
  • 180 kW practical DC charge. 210 km/h is a healthy-SoC system ceiling.
State 4

At ~8% SoC the pack is protected.

105 kW HV DC still holds 175+ km/h on the flat.

Battery discharge for traction is locked. The battery stays on the bus — no generator-only bypass. 100–120 km/h is the diesel *efficiency* comparison, not the power limit. Grade, headwind, and a punchy overtake are what break 175+.

Competition

Same tax room as Tomahawk. Better EV product.

  • ~2× the Tomahawk pack and EV range.
  • 180 kW DC vs a 25 kW vehicle charge ceiling.
  • Always-electric drive. Series loses on highway km/l — that is structural, and stated.
Today’s GST · 40%

Factory cost sits next to Tomahawk. No tax gift required.

Gearbox / DHT deletion nearly pays for ~20 kWh of extra LFP. Net factory delta: at par to ~₹0.5 lakh more at scale. Aggressive RWD retail: at par to ~₹1.4 lakh above a ~₹27.8 lakh Tomahawk-class PHEV.

Planning-basis RWD is ~₹30.1–32.2 lakh ex-showroom. AWD is a different conversation.

Policy option · 18%

A middle slab. Not the 5% EV rate.

  • Always-electric drive, no engine-to-wheel path.
  • Generator below 1,500 cc — the 1.5 qualifies; a 2.0 does not.
  • Certified EV range ≥ 270 km MIDC (planning: ~340 / ~408 km).

At 18% the RWD lands on the top-diesel shelf. Exchequer still collects ~₹3.87 lakh; 40% on a niche sticker collects approximately nothing.

Control law

One interacting system.

  • Battery answers the pedal. Generator answers sustained demand.
  • Smallest sufficient band. Dwell from when demand fell.
  • Economy 15→30%. Sport / Hills 20→35%. Sport can be refused.
  • Battery never electrically bypassed. V2L home backup never starts the engine.
Residuals

Open web can name the parents. It cannot close the third vehicle.

  • 1.5 L + PMSG in an INGLO nose designed as crush structure.
  • 42 L tank in a floor designed around a 79 kWh pack.
  • Generator start in a silent cabin. NVH is the thinnest cousin carry-over.
  • Type approval that is neither ZEV nor 7XO ICE. GST box still a policy switch.
