## Short (post this)

What if the XEV 9S could go anywhere — without a charging stop deciding the answer?

I spent months on a public engineering concept for a range-extended electric (REEV) derivative of Mahindra’s INGLO skateboard.

Not a new invention. Series REEVs already exist. The question was narrower: can India get a plug-in, always-electric-drive three-row SUV on a production 400 V platform, at a price that can sit next to a diesel or a PHEV — without a clean-sheet car?

The short version:

• Daily driving is electric. The engine never turns a wheel.
• The generator is a 1.5 L mStallion TGDi, recalibrated for fixed-band electrical duty — not the XUV 7XO’s 2.0.
• At ~8% battery the pack is protected and does not supply traction. On the concept’s road-load model, 105 kW from the generator still holds 175+ km/h on the flat.
• Factory cost, at scale, lands next to the MG Hector Tomahawk PHEV at today’s 40% GST.
• An 18% middle GST — between the 5% EV slab and 40% hybrid slab — gated on 270 km certified EV range, a sub-1.5 L generator, and no engine-to-wheel path, is what would put it on the same sticker as a top diesel. That is a policy option, not a prediction.

This is an independent concept. Nothing has been tested on hardware. Numbers are first-principles planning ranges. I directed the work myself — including using AI models as research and drafting tools — and I own every claim.
